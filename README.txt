AS Computer Science Paper 1 Practice - Offline Edition

1. Extract this ZIP to a folder.
2. Double-click demo.html. If your browser blocks the redirect, click the link shown on that page.
3. The app opens in your default browser and works offline. No Python, Node, server, or developer tools are needed.

The bundle contains 1,612 practice items and 75 question-paper / mark-scheme pairs (150 PDFs). It includes all images referenced by the live question and answer data. Responses are held only in page memory and are not saved. Seventeen known answer items currently have no usable answer resource; the app labels these when opened.

For PDF display, select Original PDF and use its link to open the local question paper at the relevant page in your browser's PDF viewer.
