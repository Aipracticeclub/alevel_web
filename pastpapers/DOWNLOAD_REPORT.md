# Cambridge Computer Science past-paper download report

Requested interval: 2012–2026 inclusive. Checked on 24 September 2026.

Actual files are retained in syllabus/year folders and in two syllabus ZIP archives.

## Verification

- Reopened every question paper and mark scheme, extracted the first two pages, and matched syllabus/component, year, session and document type to the filename.
- Recorded file sizes, SHA-256 checksums, local paths and source URLs in `inventory.csv` and the syllabus manifests.
- `pairs.json` provides paired local files and source URLs for the next website indexing step. Knowledge-point classification is not claimed by this download audit.
- Original PDF bytes are preserved. Some older PDFs have repairable internal cross-reference errors; successful parsing does not imply every page has been visually reviewed.
- ZIP CRC integrity checks: 9608: pass, 9618: pass.

## Coverage boundaries and recovered gaps

- 9608: June/November 2015–2021. Source-specific missing files and damaged copies were checked against alternate public archives.
- 9608 June 2020 /32 is not listed in the checked PapaCambridge, XtremePapers or BestExamHelp collections. It is not counted as an available pair; completeness means the available papers in the checked catalogs, not proof that every conceivable component was published.
- 9618: June/November 2021–2025 and June 2026. November 2021 offered ten components; /33 and /43 are not expected, as confirmed by the published grade thresholds.
- November 2026 is after the retrieval date and cannot be included yet. These syllabuses use June/November sessions; generic website March notices are not evidence of a March paper.
- PapaCambridge was the initial source. Its malformed 2020 link and mislabelled 2022 listing were investigated; alternate sources were used with user approval. Each downloaded file retains provenance.

Supplemental coverage: inserts and practical files are included for 9618. The 9608 collection includes 89 available supplementary PDFs; its June 2015 PM43 was not recovered, and full pre-release coverage is not asserted. These do not affect the QP/MS pair counts above.
